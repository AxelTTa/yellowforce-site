Questions

The short version.

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no ads, no forms and no accounts. Fonts and scripts load from our own site, so your browser doesn’t contact anyone else when you visit. We only get personal information when you email us.

Who we are.

This policy covers yellow-force.com, run by YellowForce, San Francisco, California. Questions about it go to .

What we collect.

  • Emails you send us. Your email address, your name if you include it, your message and anything you attach.
  • Hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, which runs that service, may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes. GitHub handles that data under its own privacy statement.
  • Copy buttons. The buttons that copy our email address write to your clipboard. They don’t read your clipboard and don’t send anything anywhere.

We don’t collect anything else from this site.

How we use it.

We use what you send us to reply, to provide services you ask for, to keep normal business records, to protect our systems and to meet legal obligations. We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it or use it for advertising.

Who else sees it.

Our email runs on Google Workspace and this site runs on GitHub Pages, so those providers process data on our behalf. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a successor if YellowForce is ever merged or acquired. Other service providers that help us run the business may also see it, under confidentiality. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it.

We keep emails as long as we need them for the conversation and our business records, then delete them. Ask us and we’ll delete yours sooner, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights.

You can ask us what we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it or stop using it. Email . Depending on where you live, for example California, the EU or the UK, the law gives you these rights. We honor them for everyone, and we won’t treat you differently for using them.

Do Not Track.

We don’t track visitors across sites, so Do Not Track signals don’t change anything here.

Children.

This site isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Where your data goes.

We’re based in the United States. What you send us is processed in the US and wherever our providers (Google, GitHub) operate.

Changes.

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the effective date at the top.
