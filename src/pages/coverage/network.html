Network.

Your network is the road every attack travels. We watch every connection on it, 24/7, and cut off anything that doesn’t belong.

The threat.

Attackers scan for exposed services, weak VPNs and unpatched edge devices, then slip in and move sideways until they reach something valuable.

Once inside, they hide in normal traffic: remote access tools, encrypted tunnels, DNS. AI makes every step faster. Agents scan for a new flaw soon after it goes public, test thousands of entry points in parallel and shape their traffic to look like your own users. Ransomware crews and swarms run the same playbook. The time between a flaw going public and someone trying it on you keeps getting shorter.
