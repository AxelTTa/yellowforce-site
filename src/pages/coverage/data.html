Data.

Data is what attackers want. We know where yours lives, who touches it and how to get it back.

The threat.

Ransomware crews now steal first and encrypt second, then threaten to publish. Some skip encryption entirely and go straight to extortion.

They hunt for your backups and delete them before you notice. Data also leaks the quiet way: files shared with anyone who has the link, exports to personal accounts, sensitive documents pasted into AI tools. AI helps attackers sort stolen data fast to find what hurts most, then write ransom notes and leak threats aimed at your customers and regulators.
