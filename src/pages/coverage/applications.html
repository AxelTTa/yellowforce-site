Apps and APIs.

Your apps and APIs face the internet all day. We attack them first, fix what we find and watch them 24/7.

The threat.

Every public app and API is a door attackers can test as often as they like. Broken access controls let one user read another user’s data.

Injection flaws, exposed admin panels, forgotten test endpoints and vulnerable open-source packages do the rest. AI agents now crawl your app, read your JavaScript, map every endpoint and try thousands of variations without getting tired. They chain small bugs into a real breach, long before a yearly test would catch it. Supply chain attacks hide in the packages your code trusts.
