Email.

Email is still where most attacks start. Now the sender can be an AI that writes like your CEO and talks like your CFO.

The threat.

Phishing used to be easy to spot. Not anymore. AI writes flawless messages in any language, studies your people through their public profiles and replies in real time like a human would.

Business email compromise hijacks a real vendor thread and swaps the bank details on an invoice. Deepfake voice and video calls impersonate executives to rush a payment or a password reset. QR codes, shared document links and fake sign-in pages carry the attack past basic filters. One click or one wire transfer is enough.
