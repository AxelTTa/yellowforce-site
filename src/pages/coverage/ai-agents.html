AI Agents.

AI is now the attacker and the target. We stop AI swarms and keep your own AI agents from being turned against you.

The threat.

Two problems at once. Outside, attackers run swarms: many AI agents working together, each scanning, phishing or exploiting in parallel and sharing what they learn.

They move at machine speed and never sleep. Inside, your own AI is a new attack surface. Prompt injection hides instructions in an email, a web page or a document, so your assistant leaks data or takes actions it shouldn’t. Poisoned tools and plugins, agents with too much power, leaked model keys and AI apps your staff started using on their own all open new doors.
