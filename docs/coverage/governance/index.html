Governance.

Security only holds if the rules behind it hold. We write, run and update yours as your company changes.

The threat.

The risk here is quiet. Policies nobody follows, access nobody ever reviewed, vendors with more access than anyone remembers, an audit that turns up gaps right before a big deal closes.

New AI rules and security questionnaires keep coming, and customers expect proof. AI also adds risks of its own: which tools your staff use, what data goes into them and who is accountable when an agent acts. Without someone in charge, all of this drifts until it shows up as a breach, a fine or a lost customer.
